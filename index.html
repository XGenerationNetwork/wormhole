About this billboard

Through a wormhole: a throat of light you fall along, the walls a plasma of noise turning as you go, speed-lines streaming past, and at the far end the exit as an Einstein ring - the stars of wherever you are going bent into a circle round the mouth, which is what a lens that strong does to whatever is behind it. The tunnel is a cylinder in polar coordinates with the noise fed cosines of the angle so there is no seam; the ring is a bright annulus with the star field mapped round it, and it grows as the exit nears, then you are through and it begins again.

Everything you see behind the headline is computed on your graphics card from a few hundred lines of code: no image, no video, nothing downloaded except three.js itself. Resize the window and it recomposes; turn on reduced motion in your system settings and it holds a single still frame.

How it is made

  • Generated, not loaded

    Shape comes from geometry and maths; surface comes from the fragment shader. There is no texture to fetch and nothing to cache.

  • Driven by a clock

    Every motion is a function of time computed on the GPU, so there is no particle array to update and nothing for the page to do each frame.

  • Disciplined

    Pixel ratio capped at 2, paused when scrolled away or when the tab is hidden, a still frame under reduced motion, released when the page is left, and a plain gradient where there is no WebGL.

Reuse it

Copy the block that starts <!-- 5OS billboard into any hero section that is position: relative, keep the two .billboard rules from the stylesheet, and put your own headline after it. Change the words in this file and it is your page.
